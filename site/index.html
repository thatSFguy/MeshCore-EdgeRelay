Personal Relay

A relay you leave in a parked car. It gives your own companions one extra hop into the mesh, and stays quiet otherwise: it forwards only what your companions send, copies messages meant for them back down to them, and drops everything else. It never advertises itself, so the mesh never learns a route through a moving car. Optional home detection pauses it while you're parked at home.

K.I.S.S. Messenger

A LilyGo T-LoRa V2.1-1.6 with an M5Stack CardKB keyboard, a buzzer and a vibration motor: read and type messages right on the device, no phone needed. It works like the app's simple mode: chat on a group channel, and set up a private DM by requesting one in the group chat.

What you need

  • A USB data cable (some cables only charge).
  • Desktop Chrome or Edge to flash ESP32 boards and to configure. Phones and Firefox/Safari can browse this site and download firmware, but can't talk to USB devices.
